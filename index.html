Research

What I Study

How vision transformers route global information through a few anchor tokens.

Anchors That Move Without Collapsing: Exclusive-Region Anchor Routing for Vision Transformers arXiv 2026
Sole author · arXiv:2611.27855 · abstract accepted at IRIS'27, IIT Madras
  • Showed in a controlled study of six anchor designs that soft anchor routing collapses (pooling-map similarity 0.99); an exclusive partition is 2.4–3.6 points more accurate than Representative Attention on CIFAR-100, matched component by component.
  • Proposed focus anchors, placed at learned importance peaks while keeping exclusive regions: +1.5 points (95% CI [0.5, 2.6]) over fixed partitions on cluttered images; on full-HD traffic signs they match dense windowed attention at ~1/5 of the FLOPs.
  • Ran 100+ training runs on rented A100s with paired-CI evaluation and reported the negative results (ResNet-18 wins from scratch; anchors never save memory). Code and logs are public.
